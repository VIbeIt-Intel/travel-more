About me

FransMari Grant

Founder of Travel More

I'm FransMari Grant, founder of Travel More, where we create travel experiences that are seamless, luxurious, and unforgettable. My journey began in 2015 at Flight Centre Retail, followed by Flight Centre Independent in 2017, and in 2025 I rebranded as Travel More to focus on bespoke family and golf escapes, and much more. I hold an Honours Degree in Tourism Management, combining professional expertise with a deep personal passion for travel.

At Travel More, we believe that every journey should be tailored to the individual. We invest in travel ourselves to ensure we provide the best advice, value, and insider tips, and we partner with the most reputable operators and wholesalers worldwide. Our foundation is built on relationships, and we take pride in offering a personal touch to ensure every client's dream holiday meets their specific needs.

While we specialise in tropical island escapes across Mauritius, the Maldives, Zanzibar, the Seychelles, Bali, and Thailand, our services extend far beyond. From cruises, ski trips, and flights to local travel, destination weddings, and corporate or incentive trips, we offer a wide range of experiences, all curated with care, expertise, and attention to detail.

With Travel More, your holiday isn't just a trip. It's a collection of moments, memories, and experiences designed to last a lifetime.
